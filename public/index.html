Include Petfinder listings (optional, uses the Claude in Chrome extension)

Petfinder has no public API, so Claude in Chrome browses Petfinder for you in your own browser. You then paste what it found here, and those pets get ranked along with everything else.

  1. Fill in your preferences above, then
  2. Open a new tab at petfinder.com, open the Claude side panel from your Chrome toolbar, paste the instructions, and send them. Let Claude finish browsing.
  3. Copy Claude's whole reply (the JSON list of pets) and paste it here:
